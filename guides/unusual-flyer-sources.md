# Unusual paper flyers and reuse rights

Reviewed 25 November 2026. A flight claim below refers to the cited model and conditions. A demo shows what happened in one setup. A published build documents construction. Controlled measurements apply to the tested configuration. Open Flight prototypes need their own build and flight records.

## Builds for the workshop

| Build | Provenance, evidence and asset decision |
| --- | --- |
| **Ring Wing** | NASA/JPL documents a whole US Letter sheet, with a tuck and optional tape, and proposes measuring travel as ring diameter changes. George Allison popularized a ring-wing variant. The 1990 *Of Wings and Things*, printed p. 70, already documents a ring-wing activity. Use Open Flight's own words, drawings and print layout with a reference credit. The separate teaching PDF credits Carlo Cayetano and stays a source link. [JPL project](https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/edu/resources/project/make-a-paper-glider/) · [JPL lesson](https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/edu/resources/lesson-plan/the-ring-wing-glider/) · [Cayetano PDF](https://d2pn8kiwq2w21t.cloudfront.net/documents/ringwing_template.pdf) · [1990 teaching guide](https://files.eric.ed.gov/fulltext/ED360170.pdf) |
| **Maple samara** | Gregory Vogt's activity, edited by NASA Glenn's Roger Storm, documents a cut outline, a paper clip and a slight curve. It asks learners to vary clip position until the model autorotates. Use an original outline and directions with a source credit. Keep Vogt's template and Dybkjær's folded designs as linked references. The workshop's red-maple seed photograph is separately licensed CC BY 3.0 by Dcrjsr. Credit the title, author, source and license, and name any changes. [NASA Glenn activity](https://www.grc.nasa.gov/www/k-12/TRC/Aeronautics/Maple_Seed.html) · [Photo and license](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Acer_rubrum_seed_keys.jpg) |
| **Williams tandem glider, Model B** | Archibald Williams's 1918 *Things To Make*, “Paper Gliders,” Fig. 128, describes two sets of lifting surfaces in tandem on one cut-and-fold sheet. This is a documented historical precedent for the one-sheet tandem, with published build guidance and an Open Flight prototype that needs physical flight trials. Use the public-domain historical source for the adaptation and credit Williams. [Historical model and Open Flight dimensions](williams-tandem.md) · [Book](https://dev.gutenberg.org/ebooks/14664) |

## Record and championship planes (Open Flight versions, reviewed 26 November 2026)

Open Flight folds four of these designs as its own versions with new names: **Needle** (Red Dart), **Sky Raft** (Shaheen Shin), **Homecoming** (Crescent Boomerang) and **Snub Nose** (Sky King). No patent or licence was found for any of them. Every surface names the original plane and its designer, links the tutorial and sources, and states that the diagrams, text and art are original Open Flight work drawn from the fold geometry, that the fold sequence is derived from the credited design, and that nothing is copied from the videos or books. Red Bull, Guinness and the original plane names appear only as factual references, with no endorsement. The fold geometry carries no Open Flight licence (the credit line reads "Folds derived from the original"); the diagrams and lessons are CC BY-SA 4.0. The planes appear in the airplane studio, the book (plates A5–A8) and the Flight Book.

| Model | Evidence and reuse decision |
| --- | --- |
| **Red Dart — Lazar Krstić** | Won distance at the Red Bull Paper Wings World Final on 14 May 2022 with 61.11 m. Guinness lists this as the championship's farthest throw. "Red Dart" is Foldable Flight's name for the plane. Foldable Flight publishes the tutorial, a free line template and a paid template; none states a licence. Open Flight draws its own diagrams and links the tutorial. [Red Bull results](https://www.redbull.com/us-en/red-bull-paper-wings-world-final-results) · [Guinness](https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/world-records/729614-farthest-throw-at-the-red-bull-paper-plane-championship) · [Tutorial](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4D6HZRaVFF8) |
| **Shaheen Shin — Shin Moo Joon (per tutorial)** | Rana Muhammad Usman Saeed (Pakistan) won airtime with it in 2022, at 14.86 s. Guinness lists his 16.39 s pre-elimination throw as the championship's time-aloft record. The designer credit appears only in the tutorial, so Open Flight credits it as reported there. [Guinness](https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/world-records/729617-longest-time-aloft-at-the-red-bull-paper-wings-championship) · [Tutorial](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lIBeQDtJLf4) |
| **Crescent Boomerang — designer not established** | Seunghoon Lee (South Korea) used it in his winning aerobatics routine in 2022, scoring 46 points. [WePlay 2018](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qP8FrPScgos) · [Tutorial](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PAp6CToFr3Y) |

Toda publishes the design in books and through his association, and keeps some details private. Open Flight's version follows the public tutorials, not his books. [Toda's channel](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YWItCyvV_AE) · [PPO tutorial](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JV2aMbGtmZE) |

Its middle shaping steps are hidden behind the folder's hands in the only source, so they could not be reconstructed. [Video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5xmG9_5r-QI) |

## Other configurations

- Everard Cunion's **paper Rogallo wing** uses two overlapping sheets. His build documents a Rogallo-inspired asymmetric glider and its launch sensitivities. Use it as a linked two-sheet comparison. [Designer article](https://everardcunion.wordpress.com/articles/paper-rogallo-wing/)
- Marc Kirschenbaum's **Biplane II** is a one-sheet display fold. The designer explicitly says it will not fly. [Designer gallery](https://sakuraorigami.com/models/vehicles/)
- The **split-level A4 tandem jet** has a contemporary maker video and limited provenance. The **joined-wing and box-wing** examples have full-scale engineering sources. A documented one-sheet, fold-only free-flight example remains unverified here. Keep all three as research references. [Tandem video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0CrAUCDScCA) · [Wolkovitch's joined-wing paper](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19860043821)

## Open assets and release order

- For the samara's science, Fig. 2 in Schaeffer, Truman, Truscott and Dickerson (2024) documents the natural seed's flight geometry and the experiments. The workshop includes the original figure PNG with the authors and CC BY 4.0 beside it. Cooper Hewitt's *Acer Saccharinum* print, Mlle. Janinet after Pierre Joseph Redouté, is a CC0 art candidate. [Scientific article and license](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10907639/) · [Cooper Hewitt record](https://www.si.edu/object/acer-saccharinum:chndm_1961-105-351)
- A classic cut rotor drawing by Theresa Knott is available under CC BY-SA 3.0, which carries attribution and share-alike terms for adaptations. For a future tumbling activity, Dupleich's measured free-fall tests on elongated paper and card rectangles provide a public-use source for a plain-strip experiment. [Rotor artwork](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Paperhelicopter2.png) · [NACA memorandum](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20050029433)
- Start with the Ring Wing's choice of diameter and the samara's clip-position experiment. Follow with the adult-assisted, cut tandem and a straight-glide trial. Mark the Open Flight builds as prototypes until paper stock, dimensions, trim, launch and results are recorded. Place attributed creator demos in a reading list for families who want to explore tumbling, passive flapping and returns.
- For any licensed inclusion, identify the designer, diagrammer, publisher and any photo or video holder. Request written permission naming the exact model and assets, modified artwork and translations in English, Spanish and Russian, editable SVG and printable PDFs, web and offline or printed Flight Book distribution, and both free and commercial use. Agree credit, license, geography, duration and any limits on further remixes before including those assets.
