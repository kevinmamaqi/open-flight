# Flight Book origins and people

The atlas uses city, habitat and historical-event markers. The city pins identify the area where a prototype was made or tested. Butterfly pins identify observed habitat or part of a migration. The three existing workshop photographs are examples of adult butterflies and have no verified connection to the map markers. Full specimen photograph credits remain in the [field notes](../NOTICES.md).

## The four civil aircraft

| Model | Place and era | Historical claim and source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Boeing 747 | Everett, Washington, United States · 9 February 1969 | The prototype was the first airplane produced in the new Everett factory. Its broad twin-aisle cabin created the wide-body passenger category. The raised cockpit made the hump. Boeing identifies Joe Sutter as design lead. The Museum of Flight finding aid identifies Jack Waddell as the first-flight pilot. [Boeing Everett story](https://www.boeing.com/features/2025/04/mount-rainier-our-beloved-backdrop) · [Boeing aircraft history](https://www.boeing.com/content/dam/boeing/v2/company/history/pdf/Boeing_Products.pdf) · [Waddell archive](https://archives.museumofflight.org/repositories/2/resources/800) |
| Douglas DC-3 | Santa Monica, California, United States · 17 December 1935 | The date is the first flight of the Douglas Sleeper Transport (DST), the first DC-3 version. The DC-3 family made passenger transport profitable for airlines. The family message distinguishes the overnight berths from the later seated version. [Smithsonian aircraft record](https://airandspace.si.edu/collection-objects/douglas-dc-3/nasm_A19530075000) · [National Postal Museum](https://postalmuseum.si.edu/exhibition/fad-to-fundamental-airmail-in-america-historic-airplanes-commercial-aircraft/dc-3) · [Douglas manufacturer history via Boeing](https://www.boeing.com/content/dam/boeing/v2/company/history/pdf/Boeing_Products.pdf) |
| Ilyushin Il-62 | Moscow, USSR, now Russia · January 1963 | The pin marks prototype development in Moscow. Four engines fitted near the tail left a clean wing for a long-range passenger airplane. In designer Yuri Egorov’s account the first flight is 2 January, while the memoir of Ilyushin’s deputy Genrikh Novozhilov gives 3 January. The card preserves the month both accounts agree on. [Egorov interview](https://www.aviaport.ru/news/246613/) · [Novozhilov memoir](https://www.mashin.ru/files/pol_05_1_60.pdf) |
| Tupolev Tu-144 | Zhukovsky, USSR, now Russia · 31 December 1968 | The first passenger airplane designed for supersonic flight to take off did so with Eduard Elyan as pilot. Its delta wing had a complex leading edge. It went through the sound barrier during tests in June 1969. [TsAGI research institute](https://www.tsagi.ru/pressroom/news/5979/) · [Tupolev published history](https://www.aviationunion.ru/upload/iblock/9e0/ljt40oomk4qwdv1zp8zwdjzlq3ji0jga/PAO-Tupolev-na-sayt.pdf) |

## The three butterflies

| Butterfly | Map anchor | Ecology and naming |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monarch (*Danaus plexippus*) | Sierra Chincua, Michoacán, Mexico | Sierra Chincua is one documented central Mexican wintering site. The eastern North American population migrates south in autumn. U.S. Fish & Wildlife Service documents other breeding and overwintering patterns, plus populations beyond North America. Caterpillars eat milkweed and the migrating adults use nectar sources. [U.S. Fish & Wildlife Service](https://www.fws.gov/species/monarch-danaus-plexippus) · [CONABIO](https://biomx.biodiversidad.gob.mx/especies/espPrioritaria/monarca/travesia) |
| Blue morpho (*Morpho peleides*) | Guanacaste, Costa Rica | The conservation area lists this name and documents Costa Rican forest habitat and feeding on fallen fruit. The Natural History Museum explains the optical structure of the wings and the ambiguity of the common name. On 24 November 2026 the [Catalogue of Life ChecklistBank record](https://api.checklistbank.org/dataset/3/nameusage/KHIXjW-JUE7OAp-PLmcPB1) returned *Morpho peleides* Kollar, 1850 with species rank and accepted status. Harvard MCZbase links *M. peleides* to *M. helenor peleides* at subspecies rank. The atlas keeps the project’s existing field label with a note about the alternative. [Natural History Museum](https://www.nhm.ac.uk/discover/spotlight-blue-morpho.html) · [Guanacaste Conservation Area](https://www.acguanacaste.ac.cr/bosque_seco_virtual/bs_web_page/paginas_de_especies/morpho_peleides.html) · [Harvard MCZ name record](https://mczbase.mcz.harvard.edu/name/Morpho%20peleides) |
| Old World swallowtail (*Papilio machaon*) | Kizhi archipelago, Karelia, Russia | The Kizhi Museum Reserve records the species on Kizhi and Bolshoy Klimenetsky, with a much wider range in temperate Eurasia, northern Africa and North America. Some of the local caterpillars use woodland angelica and milk-parsley in the carrot family. [Kizhi Museum Reserve](https://site.kizhi.karelia.ru/nature/babochki/74) |

## Two separate historical pilot stories

- **Yuri Gagarin.** The Russian State Archive of Scientific and Technical Documentation identifies the MiG-15bis as the aircraft type Gagarin reached at the final stage of training at the First Chkalov aviation school. Chkalov was the historical name of modern Orenburg. In a separate event, he launched on Vostok from Baikonur, USSR, now Kazakhstan, on 12 April 1961. The two pins identify the school city and the launch site. [Archive exhibition](https://rgantd.ru/virtual/lyudi/internet-vystavka-kakim-on-parnem-byl-k-85-letiyu-so-dnya-rozhdeniya-yu-a-gagarina-new/) · [Presidential Library](https://www.prlib.ru/history/1864421) · [Orenburg regional library](https://orenlib.ru/news/god-nauki-i-tekhnologij/ekskursija-po-mestam-gagarina.html)
- **John Glenn.** On 16 July 1957, Glenn flew a Vought F8U-1P Crusader from Los Alamitos Naval Air Station to Floyd Bennett Field for Project Bullet. The U.S. Navy and NASA cite crossing times that differ by seconds, so the story gives the duration to the nearest minute. The straight dashed map line joins the two endpoints. Glenn orbited Earth in 1962 aboard Friendship 7. [Naval History and Heritage Command](https://www.history.navy.mil/news-and-events/news/2021/nhm-071321.html) · [Ohio State University’s John Glenn archive](https://library.osu.edu/john-glenn-biographical-sketch) · [NASA](https://www.nasa.gov/history/40thmerc7/shepard.htm)

The historical pilot cards are linked contextually by country. The named people in each civil aircraft entry have roles documented for that airplane. Pilot-story records have separate IDs and no folding sheets.

## Offline assets and reuse

The public-domain [Natural Earth 1:110m land layer](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/110m-physical-vectors/) is the source for the original Open Flight basemap. Open Flight styles and credits it in English, Spanish and Russian. Source [Natural Earth Vector v5.1.2 GeoJSON](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/v5.1.2/geojson/ne_110m_land.geojson) SHA-256 is `9e0729ee253ca7d7a5c4ae9395fb1902264c5377c52e224d13dd85010e2835d9`. The SVG maps each coordinate as `x = 2 × (longitude + 180)` and `y = 2 × (90 − latitude)` rounded to one decimal at 720 × 360. The PNG preserves the same bounds for print. The base has coastlines and a 30-degree graticule. [Natural Earth terms](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/)
