The Flight Book assembles original and recolored workshop sheets at their design scale using pdf-lib. A linked entry documents each historical and ecological claim. [Atlas sources and image credits](guides/flight-book-origins.md) Its front matter embeds Open Flight Text (a renamed subset of SIL's Andika) and Alegreya, both SIL OFL 1.1, its QR code uses qrcode-generator (MIT), and its on-demand page preview uses Mozilla PDF.js (Apache 2.0). Credits and these licenses ship alongside the book art, pdf-lib, standard fonts, UPNG, pako and tslib notices. The independent motor research credits each commercial or open reference in the notebook. The downloadable fit drawing and electrical bench files carry their terms in the motor folder.
