Tailwind

Ilyushin Il-62

Swept shoulders · striped tail. Turning both tips up changes the area facing sideways. If one tip is bent more, the two sides can feel different forces. Compare equal tips first, then change one fold and track the first sideways motion.

22 mm

What you need

One rectangular sheet and a ruler. You can start with plain paper.

Print the pattern

A4 PDFUS Letter PDF

Print at 100% (Actual size) and check the 50 mm scale on the sheet with a ruler.

Try this flight test

Make one copy with flat tips and one with 10 mm tips. Give each five level launches. Record any left or right landings before changing another fold.

Twin pairs at the tail

Find the two engine pairs beside the tail and the horizontal surface above them.

Look for the pairs of stripes toward the wing tips. The bands suggest the paired engines.
