Chapter 4

Airplanes

One sheet each. Four folds, each named after a real aircraft.

Each airplane folds from one rectangular sheet. The folds decide where the paper’s weight sits and how the wings meet the air.

Upper Deck and Prairie Skipper are built to glide slowly, Tailwind to fly straight and steady, and Delta Comet is the classic dart. None has a recorded test flight yet: each plate says what to expect and what to try.
