Prairie Skipper

Douglas DC-3

Rolled leading edge · wide nose. The doubled leading edge reinforces the front of each broad wing. A bent or twisted leading edge can change the angle at which the air meets that wing. Sight across both edges before comparing your launches.

What you need

One rectangular sheet and a ruler. You can start with plain paper.

A4 PDFUS Letter PDF

Print at 100% (Actual size) and check the 50 mm scale on the sheet with a ruler.

Try this flight test

Open the wings into a very shallow V. Compare five gentle launches with five from a copy with taller turned-up tips. Count how often each lands wings-level.

Broad wing and twin propellers

The wide metal wing carries an engine and propeller on each side.

Pinch the double edge and look for the broad outline and fan-shaped stripes.
