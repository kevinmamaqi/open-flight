The book · in preparation

One Sheet

Make it fly, find out why

Fourteen flying things, each from one sheet of paper: eight airplanes, three butterflies and three flying objects. How to make them, how they fly and where their ideas come from. Free to read and print.

Read the free web edition

Look inside

Pages from an earlier preview of the web edition, in English. Photographs from Pixabay; drawings from the printable geometry.

Inside the printed edition

Chapters 1 to 5 each start with something to make: a plain-paper dart, a ring, a maple spinner and a paper butterfly. You fly it, read what happened, change one thing and fly it again. Chapters 6 and 7 use what you made. An annex gives all fourteen models a page of their own. The longer chapters on nature, history, space and contests stay in the free web edition.

The edition

Format
210 × 279 mm. It also prints at 100% on A4 and US Letter.
Pages
128 in the current proof, sewn binding planned. The final extent is set with the publisher.
Reader
Written for ages 8 to 12 reading on their own. Younger children build with a helper, and adults go deeper.
Languages
English, Spanish and Russian. The Spanish and Russian texts are drafts awaiting a native-speaker review.
Status
The English manuscript and layout are complete. Flight tests, family and classroom trials and photography come next.
Rights
Everything already published on this site keeps its open license. Terms for the printed edition are agreed with its publisher.

Every design in the book will be built, flown and photographed before the book is printed. Until then, each plate says which numbers are estimates.

We are looking for a publishing partner for the printed edition, starting with Spanish, and for schools, museums and makers who want to test it.
