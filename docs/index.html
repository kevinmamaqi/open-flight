The paper flight workshop

Fold a glider. Change its flight.

A free design-and-fly workshop for kids aged 5–12 and their grown-ups. Turn one sheet into a butterfly or airplane, then change a fold and see where it lands.

Cut + fold

Butterflies

Cut one outline, match the wingtips and fold a body you can hold. The tabletop sets the wings level. Each pattern takes its shape and colors from a real butterfly.

Airplanes

Start with plain paper. Follow the creases, shape the wings and fly a design inspired by a real airliner.

Side by side

Two gliders. One experiment.

Compare the wings, materials and folds of two designs. Print a scorecard and mark where each one first touches the floor.

Compare designs ↗Try the flight circuit ↗

At the kitchen table or in class

One fold is an experiment.

Pick a landing spot. Predict what will happen. Try a gentle glide, change one thing and launch again from the same place.

Take the family lesson ↗

Ages 5–7

Let a grown-up help with scissors or a tight crease. Point to where you think the glider will land.

Ages 8–12

Draw your prediction and compare two settings. Which changed where the glider landed?

Grown-ups

Ask what the next flight could tell you. Let the builder choose the next change.
