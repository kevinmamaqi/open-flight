Cut and fold

Blue morpho

Trace the broad blue forewing and rounded hindwing. Both sides fold up from the narrow body. Seen from the front, its wings form the steepest V of the three templates. Angled wings can respond differently to sideways airflow. Check both wingtip heights before looking for a tendency to roll.
