Chapter 5

Butterflies

Three paper gliders. Cut, fold, clip and trim, then tell us how they flew.

Real butterflies flap, twist and glide. A paper butterfly can only glide, so it must balance like a flying wing.

Build one, balance it on the dots and tell us how it flies.
