Cut and fold

Old World swallowtail

Find the two long tails behind the yellow wings. The adjustable tabs sit closer to the body. The long tails are part of the outline. Bend one of the smaller tabs inboard of them and the two wings meet the air differently. Track the first bank and the landing separately.

Open the workshop

What you need

Printed sheet, scissors, ruler and a small blunt paper clip

Print the pattern (K1)

A4 PDFUS Letter PDF

Print at 100% (Actual size) and check the 50 mm scale on the sheet with a ruler.

Try this flight test

Start with both tabs level. Raise one tab a little on a second run. Which side receives more first landings?
